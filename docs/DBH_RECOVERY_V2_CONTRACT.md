Wire, clearance and risk remain untouched. DBH evidence remains separate. Bind
the complete current journal, current final member arrays, source/preparation,
identity, candidate geometry and exact source indices. Reject stale V1 provenance.

Historical stem labels are search hints only. New ownership requires support from
the current final tree's low geometry, footprint, and a continuous raw stem that
connects to those surviving members. A crown with no surviving low connection
cannot inherit its former stem merely because a historical seed exists. Exclude
every recorded REMOVE_POINTS original_index before candidate fitting or publication.

undo derives its state from the entire current journal. Neighboring current trees

## Bounded search and geometry policy

At most three distinct ROI seeds per unavailable tree, derived from current low
members and optionally a historical axis only after current geometric support.
Each raw query retains the V1 0.60 m radius and resource caps. Reuse validated
saved raw ROIs when frame, center, source, bounds and editor proof agree.
Record all proposals, source counts, exclusion counts and candidate outcomes.

Estimate a local stem-axis proposal from robust section-center continuity. Axis
proposals may be generated from cluttered sections whose circle has enough
points/coverage but does not pass the final inlier fraction. They are proposals,
not DBH measurements. Final axis-normal fitting retains all V1 circle-quality
thresholds, including 60% inliers, 12 retained points, 180-degree coverage,
six occupied angular sectors, at least three accepted sections bracketing true
breast height, diameter bounds, diameter consistency and maximum 20-degree tilt.
Do not extrapolate a diameter from sections lying only below or above 1.30 m.

Independent column continuity may be tested in coordinates normal to the proposed
axis, using the unchanged 0.02 m/four-section V1 filter. No winning-circle residual
mask may be used to artificially pass its own final inlier-ratio check. Preserve
original XYZ/HAG/index rows; transformed coordinates are temporary fit geometry
and carry an explicit axis/breast-height coordinate definition.

Rank candidates deterministically. Multiple plausible independent stems for one
current tree are ambiguous, not a license to select the most convenient diameter.
No point can belong to two finalized DBH identities. Keep the V1 allometric
minimum of ten independent reliable trees and validation gates. Below ten,
estimated count must remain zero.

Current attachment uses an axis tube of radius DBH/2 + two V1 center-residual
bounds (2*0.03 m) + one V1 continuity voxel (0.02 m). Require at least twelve
surviving current points in three consecutive half-metre HAG bins within 2–5.5 m.
Also require raw bridge support (twelve points per 0.25 m bin from 1.30 to 2.05 m),
so an isolated lower stump cannot inherit a disconnected overhead crown. The
same attachment test against another current tree rejects competing ownership.
These are conservative association guards, separate from unchanged DBH quality.
Up to two circles per horizontal section may propose axes (twelve preliminary
fits total), allowing a secondary stem to trigger ambiguity; proposal masks never
alter final fit acceptance. Deduplicate the same physical axis within 0.15 m.
